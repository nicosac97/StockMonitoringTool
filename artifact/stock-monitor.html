    Export

    Keep a backup, or move your data into the local app later. JSON holds everything; CSV holds holdings only.

    Import

    Load a Stock Ledger JSON export, or a CSV from your broker with columns such as ticker, shares, avg_cost, currency, price. Separators , and ; and decimal commas are understood. Rows with a ticker you already hold replace that holding.

    Delete everything

    Removes all holdings, watchlist entries and settings. Export first if you may want them back.
